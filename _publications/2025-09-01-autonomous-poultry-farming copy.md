This paper (accepted in print) reviews the current state of the UK poultry industry and explores how emerging technologies can address its most pressing challenges, including labour shortages, rising feed costs, and avian influenza outbreaks. It highlights the adoption of computer vision, artificial intelligence, and robotics for tasks such as egg crack detection, disease monitoring, disinfection, dead bird removal, and automated packing. While these smart technologies show promise in improving productivity, animal welfare, and sustainability, the paper emphasizes that further advances are needed in adaptable robotic systems, intelligent grippers, and AI-driven health monitoring to achieve the long-term vision of fully autonomous, resilient, and sustainable poultry farming.
